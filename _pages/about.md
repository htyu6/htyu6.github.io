I am a first-year PhD student in Economics at Georgia Tech. I hold an MPS in Applied Economics and Management from Cornell University and a B.A. in Economics from the University of California, Irvine.

My research interests lie in **international trade**, **environmental economics**, and **urban and regional economics**, broadly within applied microeconomics. I am particularly interested in how external shocks such as trade policy, climate, and environmental regulation reshape spatial and industrial outcomes in China and other developing economies. My recent work draws on quasi-experimental methods, including difference-in-differences, event studies, propensity score matching, and instrumental variables, as well as large administrative and transaction-level datasets.

I currently work as a Research Assistant with **Prof. Yuhang Pan** at Peking University.
