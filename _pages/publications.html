My research interests include international trade, environmental economics, and urban and regional economics.

Journal Submissions

Trade War Shocks and Urban Structural Transformation: Evidence from Chinese Cities

Haotian Yu

Revise & resubmit at China Economic Review · 2026

Studies how exposure to the U.S.–China trade war reshaped Chinese cities’ sectoral composition, documenting a relative shift toward goods production rather than a clear contraction in service output.

Extreme Heat and the Reduction of the South-Facing Premium: Evidence from Shanghai’s Housing Market

Ke Xu, Xuezheng Qin, Haotian Yu

Under review at Journal of Housing Economics · 2026

Uses Shanghai resale transactions to show that extreme heat during the listing period erodes the south-facing housing premium, particularly in buildings with weaker thermal protection.

Working Papers

The Target Illusion: Mechanical Convergence in Distance-to-Target Designs

Yuhang Pan, Haotian Yu, Wenda Zhang

Working paper · 2026

Explains how mean reversion can generate apparently policy-consistent effects when treatment intensity is defined using baseline outcomes, and develops placebo diagnostics.

Performance Measures and Local Government Behavior: Evidence from China’s Wastewater Treatment Targets

Yuhang Pan, Haotian Yu, Wenda Zhang

Working paper · 2026

Examines China’s wastewater treatment targets, finding stronger investment in treatment plants and measured performance than in complementary sewer infrastructure or downstream water quality.

Global Value Chain Exposure and the Heterogeneous Effects of the US-China Trade War: Evidence from Chinese Provincial Industry Data

Haotian Yu

Working paper · Currently on hold · 2026

Explores differences in trade-war exposure across Chinese provinces and industries, with attention to global value-chain linkages and industrial profits. The project is currently on hold.

Research Proposal

Hot and Heavy: Extreme Heat, Aircraft Payload Constraints, and International Air Cargo

Haotian Yu

Independent research proposal · Early stage · 2026

Explores whether extreme heat constrains aircraft payload and international air cargo, and how airlines might adjust. This project is at the proposal stage.
