**Authors:** Ke Xu, Xuezheng Qin, **Haotian Yu**

**Status:** Under review at *Journal of Housing Economics* (2026)

### Summary

Using resale transactions in Shanghai matched with daily weather, this paper finds that the south-facing premium becomes smaller as homes accumulate more extreme-heat days while on the market. The response is stronger for thermally vulnerable buildings and is reflected primarily in sellers’ listing prices.
