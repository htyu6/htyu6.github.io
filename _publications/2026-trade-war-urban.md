### Summary

This paper studies the effect of the U.S.–China trade war on structural transformation in Chinese cities. Cities with greater pre-war export exposure experienced a relative decline in the tertiary-sector share after 2018. In levels, the evidence points more clearly to increased secondary-sector activity than to a robust decline in services, a pattern described as passive de-servicification.
