### Summary

This paper explores how industries’ participation in global value chains is associated with heterogeneous changes in industrial profits across Chinese provinces following the 2018 U.S.–China tariffs. The analysis considers forward and backward linkages, coastal and inland provinces, and differences by industrial characteristics. This working paper is currently on hold.
