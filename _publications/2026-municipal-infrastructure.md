**Authors:** Yuhang Pan, **Haotian Yu**, Wenda Zhang

**Status:** Working paper (2026)

### Summary

China’s wastewater targets induced local governments to expand measured treatment, treatment capacity, and plants. Complementary sewer networks did not respond comparably, and the paper finds no detectable differential gains in downstream river water quality or industrial abatement. These patterns illustrate incentives created by performance indicators.
