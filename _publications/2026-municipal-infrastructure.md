**With Yuhang Pan and Wenda Zhang**

<details>
<summary><b>Paper Summary</b> (click to expand)</summary>
<br>

This paper examines whether municipal wastewater infrastructure investment under China's 11th Five-Year Plan (2006–2010) induced urban industrial transformation. Exploiting cross-city variation in pre-policy wastewater treatment gaps and policy targets, we find that the policy substantially expanded wastewater treatment capacity but generated limited changes in industrial composition. At the same time, sewer network expansion responded much less strongly, pointing to the importance of complementary collection infrastructure in translating treatment capacity into broader environmental and economic effects.
