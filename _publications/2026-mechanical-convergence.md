**Authors:** Yuhang Pan, **Haotian Yu**, Wenda Zhang

**Status:** Working paper (2026)

### Summary

When a difference-in-differences treatment intensity is constructed as the distance between a baseline outcome and a policy target, mean reversion can produce an apparent dose response even without a policy effect. The paper compares zero-coded and signed doses, examines target-specific kinks, and proposes placebo-target and placebo-date tests.
